A little touch screen that types for you.

Touch Deck is free firmware for a $15–25 touch-screen board. Plug it in over USB and it becomes a keyboard and mouse with three screens: an analog watch, a clipboard that types, and a jiggler that keeps your computer awake.

Download for Windows or Mac Read the source

No drivers. No account. Open source under GPL-3.0. Apps for Windows and macOS.

Small annoyances deserve a small fix.

None of these will ruin your week. All of them add up. Touch Deck clears them off your desk.

  • “Paste is disabled for this session.”

    Remote desktops, virtual machines, KVM consoles and BIOS screens often refuse a paste. Touch Deck doesn’t paste. It types your text as real keystrokes, so it works anywhere a keyboard does.

  • Retyping that 40-character token. Again.

    Copy a command, a serial number or a long password once. It stays on the board until you clear it, so the next time is one tap.

  • The laptop went to sleep mid-render.

    Switch on the jiggler and your machine stays awake through long renders, downloads, backups and slide decks. The mouse drifts a few pixels and always stays near where it started.

  • Another gadget, another driver install.

    The board is a standard USB keyboard and mouse. Your computer already knows what to do with it, even when you can’t install software.

  • Closed hardware that only likes one OS.

    Touch Deck runs on boards sold by lots of shops, and every line of code is open. If you stop using it, the board is still yours to reuse.

Plug it in. That’s the setup.

Over USB the board introduces itself as a keyboard and a mouse, the two devices every computer has supported for decades. There’s nothing to install for that part.

  1. Get a board

    Pick one of three supported boards. They cost about as much as lunch and ship from the usual electronics shops.

  2. Install Touch Deck

    Open the app on Windows or Mac and plug in the board. It spots the factory firmware and offers Install Touch Deck. One click, no buttons to hold.

  3. Swipe and tap

    Swipe between the watch, the clipboard and the jiggler. Your settings are saved on the board and survive updates.

Three screens, one swipe apart.

These are the boards’ real screens, drawn by the firmware’s own code.

Watch

An analog watch that actually ticks.

The second hand lands exactly on the second, driven by a hardware timer. On the 1.69″ board a tiny speaker ticks along with it. One tap mutes the tick.

  • Press the BOOT button for a stopwatch, hold it to reset.
  • The app sets the time the moment you plug in.
Clipboard

A clipboard that types.

Select text on your computer and tap Copy. Later, anywhere, tap Paste and the board types it out as keystrokes. It even allows for Caps Lock.

  • Works where paste is blocked: remote desktops, VMs, consoles, login prompts.
  • The clip lives on the board, not on your PC’s clipboard. The trash can wipes it.
  • Press Ctrl+Alt+C in any app to send your selection to the board.
Jiggler

A jiggler with good handwriting.

Switch it on and a dot drives through letter-shaped lanes, O, W, M, Z and friends, while the mouse follows a few pixels at a time. Your computer stays awake for the long render, the big download or the slide deck.

  • The pointer always stays in the small area where it started.
  • Every minute or two it opens and closes a right-click menu, then picks a new letter. You can tune or turn that off.
  • Three sizes. Turn it on and off with a tap.

A companion app on your computer.

The board works on its own as a keyboard and mouse. The app adds the bits that need your computer: grabbing the selected text for Copy, setting the clock, and keeping the firmware up to date.

Windows 10 and 11

Touch Deck for Windows

  • A live copy of the board’s screen. Click it to tap, drag to swipe.
  • Installs Touch Deck on a new board with one click, and updates it later.
  • Connect several boards at once, each in its own tab.
  • Recent clips kept in memory only. Nothing written to disk.
  • Per-user installer: no admin rights, no .NET to install. Updates are signed and checked.
macOS 14 and later

Touch Deck for macOS

A menu bar app with the same features as the Windows one: the live mirror, Copy from any app, firmware installs and signed updates. It runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

  • Lives in the menu bar, out of the Dock.
  • Finds a new board on its factory firmware and offers to install Touch Deck.
  • Copy reads your selection in any app once you allow Accessibility.

Linux: the board works as a keyboard and mouse there too. There’s no app yet, so Copy needs Windows or macOS.

Your board. Your code.

Touch Deck is free software under the GPL. You can read it, change it, and put it on as many boards as you like.

  • No lock-inThe boards are general-purpose hardware. Flash something else on them tomorrow if you want to.
  • No cloud, no accountYour clip goes from your computer to the board over a USB cable. That’s the whole journey.
  • Every OS speaks USBA standard keyboard and mouse works on Windows, macOS, Linux and most things with a USB port.
  • Built in the openFirmware, apps and tools live in one public repo, tested on real hardware. Pull requests welcome.

Questions

Do I need to install drivers?

No. The RP2040 and RP2350 boards show up as a standard USB keyboard and mouse, which every major operating system supports out of the box. The ESP32-C3 board pairs as a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse. The app is optional, and the Windows installer doesn’t need admin rights.

Will Paste work in my remote desktop or VM?

If the window accepts typing, yes. The board sends real keystrokes, so to the remote machine it looks like you typed it. It types with a US keyboard layout. On a computer set to a different layout, a few symbols may come out as different characters, and the app swaps accented letters for plain ones before sending.

Where does my clipboard text go?

From your computer to the board, over the cable, and nowhere else. The board doesn’t put it on your computer’s clipboard, and the app keeps its list of recent clips in memory only. There’s no cloud service and no account.

What is the jiggler for?

Keeping a computer awake while it works on something long: a render, a big download, a backup, a presentation. Please use it within your workplace’s rules.

Does it work on a Mac or Linux?

The board works on both as a keyboard and mouse, so Paste and the jiggler work anywhere. Filling the clipboard from your computer’s selection needs the app. The apps run on Windows 10 and 11 and on macOS 14 and later.

How much does it cost?

Touch Deck is free. You only buy the board, which is roughly $12–25 depending on the model and the shop.

How do updates work?

The app checks for new versions of itself and the firmware and offers them separately. Each update list is signed, and every download is checked against its fingerprint before anything runs. Settings on the board survive firmware updates.

Can I use it in my product, or change it?

Yes. You may use, change and share Touch Deck, and even sell boards with it. If you share it, share the source of your version under the same licence. The licence page explains it in plain words.

My board isn’t on the list. Can I add it?

Probably. The firmware is split into page drawing, touch, display and USB layers, and the round pages are already shared between two chips. Open an issue or a pull request.
